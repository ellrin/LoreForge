\section{Setting}
Let $\mathbb{P}_r$ be a target distribution over a space $\mathcal{X}$, and let
$\mathbb{P}_{\theta}$ be a model distribution with parameters $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{d}$.
A sequence $(\mathbb{P}_{\theta_t})_{t \in \mathbb{N}}$ is produced by the optimiser.
The generator $g_{\theta}$ maps a noise vector $z$ to a sample, and the critic $f_w$ has
weights $w \in \mathcal{W}$. We use the transport distance
\begin{equation}
  W(\mathbb{P}_r, \mathbb{P}_{\theta}) = \sup_{\|f\|_L \leq 1} \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathbb{P}_r}[f(x)] - \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathbb{P}_{\theta}}[f(x)],
\end{equation}
where $\|f\|_L$ denotes the Lipschitz constant of $f$. The critic is trained for
$n_{\mathrm{critic}}$ steps with clipping constant $c$ and step size $\alpha$.
\begin{equation}
  \max_{w \in \mathcal{W}} \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathbb{P}_r}[f_w(x)] - \mathbb{E}_{z \sim p(z)}[f_w(g_{\theta}(z))].
\end{equation}
