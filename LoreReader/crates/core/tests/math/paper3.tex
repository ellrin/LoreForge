\section{Model}
Each observation $\mathbf{y}_n \in \mathbb{R}^{D}$ is explained by a latent code
$\mathbf{z}_n$ through a decoder $p_{\phi}(\mathbf{y}_n \mid \mathbf{z}_n)$, where $\phi$
denotes the decoder weights. The encoder $q_{\psi}$ outputs a mean $\bm{\mu}_n$ and a
scale $\bm{\sigma}_n$. The evidence lower bound is
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{E}(\phi, \psi) = \sum_{n=1}^{N} \mathbb{E}_{q_{\psi}} [\log p_{\phi}(\mathbf{y}_n \mid \mathbf{z}_n)] - \beta \, \Delta_n,
\end{equation}
where $\beta$ is the weight of the regulariser and $\Delta_n$ is the divergence of item $n$.
